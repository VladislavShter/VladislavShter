### 👤 ABOUT ME

**Solo founder and architect of four interconnected projects dedicated to digital and financial sovereignty.** Working at the intersection of **Web3**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, and privacy protection systems.

My position is straightforward: in an era where corporations and states collect more data about an individual than they know about themselves, tools must return control to the user, not take it away. Each of my projects solves a specific part of this mission.

> ⚡ **I work alone.** Managing four products in parallel. No team, no venture rounds, no architectural compromises.

### 🛠 PROJECTS

#### 🧠 E G R E G O R
A desktop application for real-time collaboration with multiple AI models simultaneously (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek) via a unified interface powered by OpenRouter. Features include debate modes, a code-review pipeline, a confidence moderator, Context Compression, and Semantic Cache. Ready for commercial acquisition.

### 🧭 PHILOSOPHY

All four projects are bound by a single global idea: **restoring agency to the individual** in the digital realm.

| Project | What does it bring back under human control? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🧠 Egregor** | Intellectual work and interaction with AI ecosystems |
| **🏦 Sovereign Bank Web3** | Finances and sovereign personal wealth |
| **🌐 Sovereign Browser** | Web presence and digital freedom of movement |
| **🜂 Sovereign** | Personal digital space and mobile privacy |
